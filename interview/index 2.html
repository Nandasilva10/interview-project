Let's Talk Jazz 🎶

Huecheer is a second year Jazz voice, duo major in psychology. Born and raised in Minnesota, then moved to Oregon. Growing up she was around a lot of music and Jazz inspirations, creating her future almost set. Psychology was always something that spiked her insterest, she always foud the work of a psychiatrist noble and extremely helpful. Follow along as we figure out more of her Jazz history.

🌟1

Inspired by Ella Fitzgerald's "Cry Me a River"

  • Ella as jazz standard
  • Influenced bebop, scatting
🎺2

Influenced by Sarah Vaughan and Nancy Wilson

  • Admire phrasing, song conveyance
  • Inspirational approaches
💛3

Jazz means joy, Jazz emans freedom, Jazz means expression from supression

🎨4

Style not strictly jazz, diverse genre influence

  • Jazz foundational to composition
  • Enjoys multiple genres and wpuld liek to learn to sing them
🧗5

Experiential aspect hardest to learn

  • Rhythm with band difficult
  • Conveying composer's message
🌀6

Improvisation seen as spontaneous creation

  • Avoids copying scats
  • Values on-the-spot creativity
✨7

"Lush Life" by Billy Strayhorn as key influence

  • Complex, non-linear composition
  • Inspired flexible note use
🚀8

Keep jazz fun and cherished

  • Avoid artistry becoming a chore
  • Jazz as reprieve from life
🌱9

Music as storytelling, not personal diary

  • Music spans many lives
  • Writing and music cohesive

Would perform with Duke Ellington

  • Influenced repertoire and style
  • Interested in his bluesy sound and standards
